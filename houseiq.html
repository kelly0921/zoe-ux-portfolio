The Problem

Homeownership is spread across many tools and conversations. Buyers search listings in one place, message agents in another, and lose track of what to do next. On House IQ, crowded maps and agent conversations that moved off the platform made it harder for users to browse homes and stay engaged.

The Solution

I designed and prototyped the My Agent portal so buyers can contact agents about a property directly in the app. Agents, in turn, can see which homes buyers are interested in. I redesigned map marker clusters to cut visual clutter and make listings easier to browse. I also created wireframes for bringing AI chat throughout the app. I used heatmap insights and user interviews to choose where the assistant appears.
