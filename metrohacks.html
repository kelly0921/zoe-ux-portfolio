METROHACKS

  • by:Zoe Lu
  • date:December, 2019
  • T-shirt Design, Graphic Design

T-Shirt designs created for METROHACKS, one of DFW's largest, beginner-friendly, 24-hour virtual hackathons with a focus on applying cutting-edge technology in real apps.

Inspired by the dynamic energy of hackathons, the designs feature sleek, modern elements that reflect both the fast pace of tech development and the creativity involved in problem-solving. Bold colors, geometric patterns, and digital motifs symbolized the intersection of technology and imagination.

Each design is crafted to stand out, resonating with the tech-driven audience and the futuristic theme of METROHACKS. Whether worn by participants or mentors, the T-shirts serve as a badge of participation in an event that encourages collaboration, learning, and pushing the boundaries of innovation.
