Dripper's Coffee

  • by:Zoe Lu
  • date:December, 2019
  • Packaging Design, Graphic Design, Product Design

The Dripper’s Coffee bean bag design brings joy to everyday coffee experiences. The use of abstract, nonobjective shapes makes the design feel fresh and unpredictable, much like the exciting flavors of artisanal coffee. Bright colors and whimsical forms create a packaging that catches the eye and sparks curiosity.

For Dripper’s Coffee, I crafted an artistic bean bag design featuring fun, nonobjective shapes that bring a playful twist to the packaging.

From the start, I aimed to create a look that stands out on the shelf, combining patterns to reflect the unique, handcrafted quality of the coffee itself.

The process began with sketches for the logo, where I explored bold and dynamic forms, bringing the brand’s identity to life in a fun, eye-catching way.
