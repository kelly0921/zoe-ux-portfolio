The Problem

Shoppers often spot something they love in real life, like a jacket on a stranger or a lamp in a café, but have no fast way to find it online. Vague keyword searches and endless scrolling turn interest into frustration, and many people give up before they buy.

The Solution

FitFind is a visual shopping app that turns a photo into a purchase. Users snap or upload an image, FitFind identifies the brand, and they can shop the item in a few taps without leaving the app. I handled the research and UX/UI design, shaping the flow to cut the steps between “I want that” and checkout.
