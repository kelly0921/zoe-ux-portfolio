Problem Statement:

Fitfind is a project I designed to make shopping easier - users can snap a photo or upload an image of an item they want to buy, and the app will identify the brand and directly purchase it.

The Mission:

The time-consuming process of finding products online frustrates users, reducing their motivation to complete a purchase.
