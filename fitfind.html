The Problem:

The time-consuming process of finding products online frustrates users, reducing their motivation to complete a purchase.

The Solution:

FitFind is a visual shopping app that helps users discover and purchase products they see in everyday life. By uploading or snapping a photo, users can identify the brand and shop the item in just a few taps all on one platform.
