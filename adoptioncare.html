The Problem

About 12,639 children in Texas foster care are waiting to be adopted. Families who want to adopt face home studies, court hearings, legal jargon, and many parties, including CPS, CASA and agencies. They get few resources to guide them, so many feel alone in the process and fewer adoptions succeed.

The Solution

Our team of four mapped the full foster-to-adopt journey, from joining an agency to the final adoption hearing. We built personas to understand where families get stuck. We turned those insights into six guiding principles and designed Adoption Care. It’s a mobile app that walks families through each step, sets clear expectations, and connects them with resources and a support network.
